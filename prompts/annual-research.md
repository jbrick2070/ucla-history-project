# Annual Research Prompt

Replace `[YEAR]`, `[PREVIOUS YEAR]`, and attach `PROJECT-BIBLE.md`, the previous handoff, and the current source packet.

```text
You are continuing a 100-year, year-by-year history of UCLA.

Target year: [YEAR]
Previous year: [PREVIOUS YEAR]

Follow the attached Project Bible. Research only January 1–December 31 of [YEAR]. Use the previous year only to explain continuity, not as evidence for current-year claims. Do not import later events into the narrative.

Prioritize contemporary primary sources. For every important claim provide the exact date, source title, page or issue locator, direct URL, and confidence level. Quote only verbatim text. Label later retrospective evidence, uncertainty, folklore, and contradictions.

Deliver:
1. A strict calendar-year timeline.
2. An evidence matrix.
3. An exact quote bank.
4. A contradiction and folklore audit.
5. Three possible narrative spines.
6. An image/video manifest with credits and rights notes.
7. Research gaps and promising leads.
8. Material that belongs to another year and must be excluded.
9. A handoff section identifying continuities and questions for [YEAR + 1].

Do not write a polished script until the evidence audit is complete. Never invent facts, scenes, dialogue, motives, dates, quotations, or causal links.
```
