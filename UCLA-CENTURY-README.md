# UCLA Century Project

A year-by-year, primary-source history of UCLA. Each calendar year is a self-contained research packet, followed by a verified synthesis and a handoff for the next year.

## Annual workflow

1. Copy `templates/year-research.md` to `years/YYYY/research.md`.
2. Run the annual research brief prompt.
3. Run an independent Gemini deep-research pass.
4. Reconcile both reports and audit every date, quotation, and URL.
5. Use the verified packet in NotebookLM for the optional five-minute production.
6. Complete `years/YYYY/handoff.md` before beginning the next year.

The repository—not chat history or NotebookLM—is the project memory.

## Rules

- Calendar-year scope: January 1 through December 31 of the target year.
- Contemporary primary sources come first.
- Exact quotations require a source, locator, and stable URL.
- Later summaries are labeled retrospective and never silently treated as contemporary evidence.
- Contradictions, gaps, folklore, and rejected claims remain visible.
- Generated visuals are labeled; archival visuals receive credits and rights notes.
